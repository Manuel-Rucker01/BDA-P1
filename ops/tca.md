> **Caveat — paper fills are optimistic.** Alpaca's paper engine fills a market order at the prevailing quote, with no market impact, no queueing and no adverse selection. The realised columns below therefore measure (at best) the quoted half-spread at the moment of the order; they are a *lower bound* on what the same orders would cost live. The modelled column is what the backtest (`v3/costs.py`) charges for the same name and trade size at the actual account size.
